Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

At NEET Quiz Hub, accessible through our website, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used and the choices available to visitors.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse and use many parts of NEET Quiz Hub without providing personal information.

However, when you voluntarily submit a question report, feedback or suggestion through features available on our website, the information you enter may be collected and processed for the purpose of reviewing and responding to your submission.

Depending on the information you provide, this may include the issue category, question reference, feedback or other details entered into the submission form.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through our website may be used to:

  • Review reports about incorrect questions or answers.
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of educational content.
  • Understand feedback and suggestions from visitors.
  • Maintain and improve website functionality.
  • Help protect the website against misuse or technical problems.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies. Cookies are small text files stored in a visitor's browser.

These technologies may be used to support website functionality, understand website usage and, where applicable, provide advertising services.

NEET Quiz Hub may use Google AdSense or other advertising services in the future to display advertisements.

If Google AdSense is used on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to collect information resulting from the display of advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services for hosting, data processing, website functionality and advertising.

For example, the website is hosted using GitHub Pages. If website forms or feedback features use Google services, information submitted through those features may be processed by the relevant Google service.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage visitors to review those policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

NEET Quiz Hub is an educational platform intended for students preparing for competitive examinations. We do not knowingly request personal information from children beyond information voluntarily submitted through available website features.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has submitted personal information and wish to raise a concern may contact us through the website's available feedback or reporting options.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Visitors can control cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some third-party services.

Where applicable, visitors may also use the advertising preference and opt-out options provided by Google and other advertising partners.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website features, services or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the feedback or reporting options available on NEET Quiz Hub.
