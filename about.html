About NEET Quiz Hub

Welcome to NEET Quiz Hub, an independent educational platform created to help students prepare for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) through online practice and self-assessment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make NEET preparation more accessible and organised by providing students with useful practice resources that support concept revision, knowledge assessment and regular preparation.

What We Offer

NEET Quiz Hub provides educational practice resources, including:

  • Subject-wise practice questions in Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology.
  • Practice quizzes to help students revise important concepts.
  • Mock tests for self-assessment and exam preparation.
  • Answer keys and explanations to support learning.
  • A simple online platform that students can access from different devices.

Our Content

Our educational material is prepared for practice and revision purposes. We aim to provide clear questions, accurate answers and helpful explanations. We continuously work to review and improve the quality of the content.

Students are encouraged to refer to NCERT textbooks and official examination resources as their primary study materials.

Who Can Use NEET Quiz Hub?

Our platform is intended for students preparing for NEET-UG and other learners interested in practising relevant Physics, Chemistry and Biology concepts.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide a user-friendly learning experience, maintain transparent website policies and improve our educational resources based on feedback.

Independent Educational Platform

NEET Quiz Hub is an independent educational website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially associated with the National Testing Agency (NTA), the National Medical Commission (NMC), or any government examination authority.

Contact Us

If you find an incorrect question or have feedback about our educational content, please use the Report Question option available on our website.
